What is the Pomodoro Technique?

The Pomodoro Technique is a time management method developed by Francesco Cirillo in the late 1980s. It uses a timer to break work into focused intervals.

  1. Choose a task you want to work on
  2. Set the timer for 25 minutes (one "Pomodoro")
  3. Work focused until the timer rings
  4. Take a short break (5 minutes)
  5. After 4 Pomodoros, take a longer break (15-30 minutes)
